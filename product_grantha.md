# PART 1: THE PRODUCT & BUSINESS ENGINE

### 1.1 Product Vision & Executive Pitch
* **The One-Liner:** Placeme is the autonomous campus placement operating system that replaces spreadsheets, coordinator discretion, and lateral offer chaos with 20 configurable mathematical rules and a cryptographic application pipeline.
* **The Problem:** Campus placements in higher-ed institutions are catastrophic operational bottlenecks:
  1. *Dispute Chaos:* Students dispute offer tiers, eligibility exclusions, and blacklisting decisions made manually by placement coordinators.
  2. *Lateral Poaching:* Placed students continue applying for parallel side-grade offers (e.g., jumping from ₹6 LPA to ₹6.5 LPA), taking away first-job opportunities from unplaced peers.
  3. *Fragmented Tools:* Colleges juggle Google Sheets, WhatsApp groups, ERP portals, and unvetted resume PDFs.
  4. *NIRF Reporting Nightmare:* Generating mandatory NIRF/NAAC accreditation placement reports takes weeks of manual spreadsheet reconciliation.
* **The Solution:** A unified, multi-tenant platform connecting **Hiring Agencies**, **Colleges (TPOs)**, and **Students** with:
  - An autonomous 20-policy mathematical rule engine.
  - An immutable 14-stage application conveyor.
  - Multiplier-gated offer upgrades.
  - Deep AI tools for student resumes and cohort skill gap telemetry.

#### Persona 1: Enterprise Hiring Agency (SuperAdmin & Agency Staff)
* **Who they are:** Central placement consortiums, corporate hiring networks, or university system headquarters overseeing dozens of affiliated colleges.
* **Core Jobs:**
  - Onboard new colleges with custom onboarding field schemas (`years`, `degrees`, `departments`).
  - Broadcast global jobs available across multiple college campuses simultaneously.
  - Track consortium-wide placement velocity and audit compliance.
* **Top Frustration:** Untrustworthy, inconsistent student data across different college branches and lack of centralized placement compliance.

#### Persona 2: College Placement Cell / TPO (College Admin & Staff)
* **Who they are:** Training & Placement Officers (TPOs), Deans of Corporate Relations, and department placement coordinators.
* **Core Jobs:**
  - Configure the 20 institutional policies (e.g. CGPA cutoffs, no-show penalties, 1.5× CTC upgrade multiplier).
  - Review incoming applicant batches, approve shortlists, and record interview outcomes.
  - Run AI-driven cohort readiness analyses to identify skill gaps against visiting recruiter JDs.
  - Export NIRF/NAAC-compliant placement statistics.
* **Top Frustration:** Endless student disputes, students skipping scheduled interviews ("no-shows"), and coordinators being blamed for favoritism.

#### Persona 3: Final-Year Student (The Candidate)
* **Who they are:** Engineering, management, and undergraduate students pursuing campus placements.
* **Core Jobs:**
  - Build verified DigiProfiles with self-authored academic and project histories.
  - Generate job-tailored ATS-compliant resumes via LLM.
  - Discover external matched opportunities via scraped LinkedIn & Internshala feeds.
  - Track real-time placement status and unlock Dream/Super Dream job opportunities.
* **Top Frustration:** Uncertainty about eligibility, opaque rejection reasons, and rigid resume formats.

### 1.3 Core Differentiators & Unfair Advantages

1. **"The College Defines the Policy; Placeme Enforces the Math":** Unlike legacy placement software with hardcoded rules, Placeme gives colleges 20 modular policies with fully customizable thresholds, operators, penalty strikes, and cooling-period reinstatements.
2. **Autonomous Multiplier Offer Gate:** Eliminates lateral side-grades mathematically. A student with a ₹12 LPA offer is barred from applying to anything below ₹18 LPA (if a 1.5× multiplier is active).
3. **True Zero-Trust Tri-Party RLS:** Every single Postgres query is filtered at the database level using Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) driven by cryptographic JWT `app_metadata`. Tenant isolation is mathematically guaranteed.
4. **Instant NIRF Accreditation Intelligence:** No spreadsheet massaging. Real-time telemetry calculates clearance velocity, median CTC, branch breakdowns, and package distributions formatted for official regulatory review.
5. **Integrated AI Workflow Suite:** Built-in ATS LaTeX resume generation, live multi-platform job scraping (LinkedIn/Internshala via Serper), and 4-layer cohort readiness audits.

| **06** | **Offer Limits & Holding** | Limits how many concurrent offers a student can hoard. | Max total offers (e.g. 1 or 2), Max active, Coexistence rule. |
| **07** | **Offer Upgrade Policy** | Mathematically unlocks subsequent higher tier drives. | Base CTC Multiplier (1.5×, 2.0×), Min delta (e.g. +₹4 LPA), Upgrade caps. |
| **08** | **Job Types Classification** | Controls opportunities based on contract type. | FTE, Internship, PPO, 6-Month Intern, Dual-offer allowances. |
| **09** | **Placement Levels** | College-defined tier ladder (e.g. Mass, Core, Dream, Super Dream). | Level definitions, Level priority hierarchy, Progression locks. |
| **10** | **Placement Categories** | Segments opportunities by domain (IT, Core, BFSI, Consulting). | Cross-category application permissions, Category quotas. |
| **11** | **Level Movement Rules** | Governs upward, lateral, and downward level movement. | Upward only allowed; lateral blocked; downward strictly forbidden. |
| **12** | **Attempt / Interview Limits** | Caps total interview opportunities per semester/cycle. | Max technical interview attempts per company tier. |
| **13** | **Dream Policy** | Defines qualifications and perks for the "Dream" tier. | Dream CTC cutoff (e.g. ≥ ₹10 LPA), special exemption rules. |
| **14** | **Super Dream Policy** | The apex tier (e.g. ≥ ₹20 LPA); overrides lower restrictions. | Super Dream cutoff, Open-to-all placed students toggle. |
| **15** | **Special Exception Policy** | Handles sports quotas, PwD accommodations, or honors students. | Custom exception rules by student attribute or batch. |
| **16** | **Offer Acceptance Window** | Enforces countdown clock on accepting released offers. | Acceptance deadline (e.g. 48 hours), Auto-forfeit on expiry. |
| **17** | **Placement Completion** | Formally de-registers a student once placed and satisfied. | Auto-debar on Hired status, Release of NOC certificates. |
| **18** | **Training & Readiness** | Mandates mock test or soft-skills completion prior to drives. | Assessment pass requirement, Minimum mock interview score. |
| **19** | **Academic Clearance** | Real-time freeze on students who fail current semester exams. | Immediate policy lockout if active backlog occurs during season. |

# PART 2: VISUAL & DESIGN SYSTEM DNA

### 2.1 The Visual Philosophy
Placeme uses the **Verdana Health & Calm Engineering** design system. It avoids the chaotic, high-saturation, neon aesthetics of generic crypto/SaaS tools. It is built upon:
* **Deep Navy (`#0F172A`) & Warm Paper Canvas (`#F8FAFC` / `#f6f3f1`):** Represents institutional authority, stability, and clinical readability.
* **Tertiary Sage (`#059669`) & Vivid Electric Indigo (`#2B59D1`):** Strategic interactive color bursts reserved strictly for active gates, confirmed badges, and Primary CTAs.
* **Micro-Pills & Soft Surfaces:** Every button and tag utilizes `rounded-full` or soft radii (`rounded-2xl`) to evoke calm reassurance rather than rigid institutional hostility.

### 2.6 Motion Choreography & Kinetic Rules
* **Spring Dynamics:** Framer Motion spring curves: `stiffness: 300, damping: 25`.
* **State Loops:** 10-second continuous loop choreography using structured promises:
  - Step 0 (0.0s – 1.5s): Initial resting state / verified anchor.
  - Step 1 (1.5s – 4.0s): Action trigger / animated cursor movement / button depress.
  - Step 2 (4.0s – 7.5s): Visual transformation / counter flip / table row filtration.
  - Step 3 (7.5s – 10.0s): Green confirmation badge / celebratory lockup / graceful reset.

### 2.9 Screen-Level Information Architecture (IA) & Flow Engine (Beyond Reskinning)

A major failure mode in AI-driven development is **"Superficial Reskinning"**—where an agent applies design tokens (colors, border-radius, fonts) to an existing layout without rethinking the **Information Architecture (IA), visual hierarchy, and user flow**.

To build true, high-craft, page-by-page experiences that feel completely new, all page designs must follow the **3-Layer Architectural Redesign Formula** using verified, free screen reference engines.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       THE 3-LAYER REDESIGN FORMULA                          │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ LAYER 1: SKELETON (Layout & IA)   Refero.design / 21st.dev / Lazyweb        │
│                                   (Grid, columns, slide-overs, sticky bars) │
│                                                                             │
│ LAYER 2: BRAND DNA (Tokens)       product_grantha.md / DESIGN_*.md          │
│                                   (Navy, Sage, Indigo, Plus Jakarta, Pills) │
│                                                                             │
│ LAYER 3: TACTILE FEEL (Motion)    @/components/ui/tactile                   │
│                                   (Spring clicks, sliding tabs, rolling CTC)│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### The 4 Open Screen & Flow Reference Engines
Instead of paywalled tools like Mobbin, agents and engineers should anchor on these four open sources:
1. **[Refero.design](https://refero.design):** The ultimate free reference library for real SaaS web applications. Contains thousands of full-page web app screenshots from Linear, Stripe, Attio, Supabase, Vercel, and Notion. Searchable by screen type (`Data Table`, `Dashboard`, `Settings`, `Filter Bar`, `Pipeline Kanban`).
2. **[21st.dev](https://21st.dev):** The open-source registry for full-page React, Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion compositions (command palettes, slide-over candidate drawers, live analytics telemetry).
3. **Lazyweb Screen & Flow Search:**
   - Run `/lazyweb-search-screens` to find evidence-backed SaaS layout patterns.
   - Run `/lazyweb-search-flows` to study multi-step user journeys (e.g. invite-to-onboarding wizards).
4. **[SaaSframe.io](https://saasframe.io) & [PageFlows.com](https://pageflows.com):** Curated libraries tracking complete UX flows (onboarding, checkout, drive scheduling).
